# OnCampus

### Centralized College Activity & Opportunity Platform

OnCampus is a web-based platform designed to bring college activities, workshops, competitions, events, and student opportunities together in one place.

The platform provides separate experiences for **Students, Organizers, and Administrators**, making the complete activity lifecycle easier to manage.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Students often have to rely on different groups, notices, and social media pages to discover what's happening around campus.

**OnCampus** provides a centralized platform where students can:

- Discover campus activities
- Search and filter activities
- View activity details
- Register for activities
- Track their registrations
- Receive notifications

Organizers can create activities and manage participants, while administrators review and approve activities before they are published.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student

- Browse approved campus activities
- Search activities
- Filter activities by category
- View complete activity details
- Register for activities
- Cancel registrations
- View registration history
- Receive notifications
- Track upcoming activities

### 🧑‍💼 Organizer

- Create new activities
- Add activity information
- Submit activities for approval
- View created activities
- View registered participants
- Track activity approval status

### 🛡️ Administrator

- View pending activities
- Review submitted activities
- Approve activities
- Reject activities
- Control the activity publishing workflow
